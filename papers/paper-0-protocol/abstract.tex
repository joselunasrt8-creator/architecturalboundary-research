This paper specifies a protocol for investigating candidate architectural invariants: properties hypothesized to hold across families of software architectures under stated scopes and constraints. The protocol is designed to be reproducible, falsifiable, and independent of any particular invariant claim. It separates observation from theory, standardizes artifact production, and defines measurement and classification rules so that results from different research groups can be compared and aggregated. We provide a universal research pipeline, required artifacts, versioning rules, and a threat model for protocol deviations.

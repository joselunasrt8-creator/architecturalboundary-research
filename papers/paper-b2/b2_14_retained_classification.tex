\subsection{Protocol audit finding (AUD-001)}
\label{sec:protocol-audit-finding}
\textbf{AUD-001 --- Protocol v1.0 execution under-specification.}
\textbf{Finding:} The Paper~B2 registration did not contain a complete investigation-specific I4 measurement mapping and I5 decision function capable of uniquely mapping the Comparative Dataset to \{Supports, Violates, Indeterminate\}.
\textbf{Impact:} Metric-level synthesis is reproducible. Candidate RLE classification is not uniquely reproducible.
\textbf{B2 consequence:} No retained RLE classification is issued. All candidate classification rows remain unclassified due to the missing preregistered mapping.
\textbf{Future disposition:} Define I4/I5 prospectively in Protocol v1.x or the registration for Paper~B3, then execute the full investigation again under those frozen rules.

For B2-BND-001, Paper~B2 does not provide a retention or rejection rationale because no protocol outcome labels can be issued without the preregistered I4/I5 mapping (see \texttt{AUD-001}).

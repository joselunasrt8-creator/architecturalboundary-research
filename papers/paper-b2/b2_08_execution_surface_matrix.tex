\section{Execution Surface Matrix}
\label{sec:esm}
The execution surface matrix (ESM) records, per system, the externally-reachable and internally-invoked surfaces at which a request can be initiated, admitted, authorized, constrained, audited, or rejected. This section is an \emph{SRF-aligned} surface registry: it is descriptive, traceable to BORs, and intended to feed later derivations.

\textbf{Separation rule.} Each row distinguishes (a) \emph{observation} (directly stated in BOR) from (b) \emph{interpretation} (a normalization or naming choice). No derived objects, measurements, or pattern classifications may be introduced here.

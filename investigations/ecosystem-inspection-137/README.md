### Four-stage role audit

The candidate questions are useful hypotheses, not established separations.
ABR already designs procedures and normalizes structural surfaces, so it overlaps
both Methodology Engineering and Structural Foundations. Methodology Engineering
may also specify evidence structures; Structural Foundations and SYNAPSE both
need representation semantics. SYNAPSE depends on sufficiently precise,
observable structural claims, but deterministic conformance cannot validate the
claim's empirical warrant. Gaps are a canonical method-reuse acceptance record,
a canonical structural-claim envelope, and target-owner acceptance. Duplicated
responsibility is likely around schemas, invariants, and validation. Potentially
incompatible semantics include ABR's epistemic/uncertain claims versus binary
checker results and differing meanings of “invariant.” No available artifact
represents all required handoff fields. Therefore linearity, distinctness, and
four-stage necessity remain hypotheses.

## 7. Frozen determinations

### Stage-level rules

| Exact state | Sufficient evidence |
| --- | --- |
| `STAGE_UNIQUE_VALUE_OBSERVED` | At least one owner-confirmed decision-relevant incremental finding/effect absent from full native baseline and simpler counterfactual, with valid provenance and harms measured |
| `STAGE_VALUE_WITH_LIMITATIONS` | Confirmed incremental benefit exists but scope, false findings/blocks, semantic loss, burden, or unavailable evidence materially limits it |
| `STAGE_NO_UNIQUE_VALUE` | Valid completed comparison finds no incremental decision-relevant benefit; not merely zero findings |
| `STAGE_REDUNDANT` | Native or simpler method produces materially equivalent accepted findings/decision effect at no greater burden |
| `STAGE_BLOCKED` | Mandatory immutable input, execution capability, owner acceptance, or adjudication is unavailable; absence is not failure |
| `STAGE_OUT_OF_SCOPE` | Canonical stage contract excludes the target question/input, prospectively or by owner-confirmed contract |
| `STAGE_INDETERMINATE` | Admissible evidence remains conflicting or insufficient after a valid attempt and neither blocked nor out of scope applies |

### Target-level pipeline rules

| Exact state | Sufficient evidence |
| --- | --- |
| `FULL_PIPELINE_VALUE_OBSERVED` | All four stages independently meet `STAGE_UNIQUE_VALUE_OBSERVED`, each contribution is distinct, every handoff is acceptable, and aggregate harms do not defeat the decision benefit |
| `PARTIAL_PIPELINE_VALUE_OBSERVED` | One or more but fewer than four stages adds confirmed incremental value, or a non-linear/skip topology adds value; identify non-valued stages |
| `PIPELINE_REDUNDANT_WITH_NATIVE_PROCESS` | Valid full applicable comparison is materially reproduced by native process with no greater burden |
| `PIPELINE_FRICTION_EXCEEDS_VALUE` | Measured setup/execution/review cost, false findings/blocks, ambiguity, delay, or complexity outweighs accepted decision benefit under the frozen target rule |
| `PIPELINE_BLOCKED` | A load-bearing applicable stage/input/handoff cannot be tested and prevents a target determination |
| `PIPELINE_NOT_APPLICABLE` | Target-owner contracts/questions leave no justified four-stage or useful partial topology |
| `PIPELINE_INDETERMINATE` | Valid evidence cannot distinguish the remaining target-level states |

Completion alone is never value. `FULL_PIPELINE_VALUE_OBSERVED` cannot be used
if any stage is merely limited, redundant, blocked, out of scope, or
indeterminate.

### Cross-target generality rules

| Exact state | Sufficient evidence across the frozen cohort |
| --- | --- |
| `INSPECTION_PIPELINE_GENERALITY_SUPPORTED` | At least three accessible targets spanning at least three materially different classes have valid comparisons and `FULL_PIPELINE_VALUE_OBSERVED`, no tested class has contradiction/friction-exceeds-value, and handoffs/topology reproduce without target-specific semantic mutation |
| `INSPECTION_PIPELINE_PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` | Valid incremental value reproduces on at least two targets in at least two classes, but only a partial topology/stage subset generalizes or explicit limits/contradictions remain |
| `INSPECTION_PIPELINE_NOT_SUPPORTED` | At least three valid targets spanning at least three classes predominantly show redundancy/no unique stage value/friction exceeding value, with no coherent bounded class where the full sequence repeats |

One target never establishes generality. Blocked targets do not count as
negative results, and classes/targets are not silently replaced.

## 8. Handoff tests and topology alternatives

For each proposed edge, freeze producer input IDs/hashes and have the consumer
return an acceptance record before seeing downstream outcomes. Compare these
nine fields: **artifact identity, provenance, epistemic status, assumptions,
uncertainty, repository ownership, producer scope, consumer acceptance, and
authority boundary**. Each is `PRESERVED`, `CHANGED_WITH_ACCEPTED_MAPPING`,
`LOST`, `REJECTED`, or `UNKNOWN`; any unacknowledged change/loss fails the
handoff. A consumer must reference exact inputs, declare interpretation and
rejections, and must not claim producer or target authority.

| Edge | Frozen test | Specific failure probe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ABR → Methodology Engineering | Can a bounded question, rival explanations, admissible evidence, and uncertainty be accepted as method-design input without becoming a result? | Remove epistemic status or swap source hash; consumer must reject |
| Methodology Engineering → Structural Foundations | Can an accepted procedure expose required objects/relations and admissibility constraints without Structural Foundations rewriting method scope? | Present a procedure-specific convenience as domain invariant; consumer must flag/reject |
| Structural Foundations → SYNAPSE | Can each check retain source claim identity and uncertainty while declaring observable projection and oracle limits? | Supply unobservable/qualified claim as Boolean invariant; checker boundary must block rather than false-pass |

Execute matched topology alternatives where inputs permit: native-only;
independent single stage; ABR→Structural Foundations (skip Methodology);
ABR→Methodology→SYNAPSE (skip Structural only if a native structural contract
exists); Structural→ABR feedback before method selection; and the proposed
linear sequence. Compare findings and burdens. Reordering/skipping is a result
to preserve, not protocol failure. No stage may consume later-stage outcomes
when generating its input.

This section interprets the protocol artifacts (BOR/ESM/DER/MSR and the cohort comparative dataset) to evaluate the candidate Reasoning\,$\rightarrow$\,Legitimacy\,$\rightarrow$\,Execution (RLE) boundary.

\subsection{Program status: prospective execution and traceability milestone}
Conceptually, the research program now has the following executed sequence:
\begin{quote}
Paper~0 $\rightarrow$ Protocol~v1.0 $\rightarrow$ B1 (pilot execution) $\rightarrow$ B2-Run-1 (execution; preregistration incomplete) $\rightarrow$ AUD-001 $\rightarrow$ B2-Run-2 (prospectively preregistered execution) $\rightarrow$ Empirical result.
\end{quote}

If the execution and traceability are correct, then B2-Run-2 establishes: (i) a protocol that can be executed prospectively; (ii) a fully traceable evidence pipeline from BOR through the Comparative Dataset; (iii) a preregistered decision rule that yields a deterministic cohort result; and (iv) a hypothesis tested without modification after observing the evidence.

\textbf{Constraints (frozen artifacts; no new decision logic).} Analysis in Paper~B2 is performed by synthesizing values already present in the Comparative Dataset (and its trace links to MSR/DER/SRF/BOR). The analysis must: (i) not modify BOR, SRF, DER, MSR, or the Comparative Dataset; (ii) not introduce new observations, measurements, metrics, thresholds, or decision rules; (iii) ensure every claim is traceable through Comparative Dataset $\rightarrow$ MSR $\rightarrow$ DER $\rightarrow$ SRF $\rightarrow$ BOR; and (iv) if a protocol-defined conclusion cannot be reached uniquely from the registered decision rules for this investigation, record the ambiguity rather than resolving it.

\subsection{Run result (B2-Run-2)}
\paragraph{Observed measurements (data).}
Across the nine Comparative Dataset rows (DER-001--DER-009), exactly one row (DER-002) has the complete measurement vector $(1,1,1,1,1)$. The remaining eight rows have at least one component equal to 0.

\paragraph{Application of the frozen I5 decision rule (mechanical).}
Applying the registered I5 rule to the observed vectors yields:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Supports: 1/9
  \item Violates: 8/9
  \item Indeterminate: 0/9
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Cohort conclusion (outcome under the preregistered rule).}
Because there is at least one \emph{Violates} outcome and there are no \emph{Indeterminate} outcomes, the cohort outcome is \textbf{Violates}.

\subsection{Scientific meaning (interpretation layer)}
The evidence does not support the preregistered claim that every candidate in the governance cohort exhibits the complete explicit R$\rightarrow$L$\rightarrow$E structure required by I5. This does not imply that reasoning, legitimacy, and execution are irrelevant; it implies that the strict all-components invariant, as preregistered, does not hold across this cohort.

\subsection{Cohort outcome (precedence rule)}
Across the nine Comparative Dataset rows (DER-001--DER-009), there exists at least one \emph{Violates} outcome and there are no \emph{Indeterminate} outcomes. Therefore, the cohort outcome under the frozen precedence rule is \textbf{Violates}.

\subsection{Interpretation layer: candidate boundary status categories}
Given the cohort outcome \emph{Violates}, the candidate RLE boundary is retained with status label \textbf{Unsupported} for this cohort.

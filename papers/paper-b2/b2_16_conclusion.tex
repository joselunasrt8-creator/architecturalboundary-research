\section{Conclusion}
Paper~B2 applies the Invariance-Based Architectural Investigation Protocol v1.0 (Paper~0) to a governance/authorization cohort. In the current repository state, BOR and SRF artifacts are present and two canonical DER reference executions are registered; the MSR, Comparative Dataset, Analysis, and Retained Classification stages have not started.

Remaining DER execution, MSR execution, and Comparative Dataset construction are incomplete, and the Paper~B2 protocol registration did not preregister a sufficiently explicit investigation-specific I4 measurement mapping and I5 decision function to uniquely map a future Comparative Dataset to the Protocol v1.0 outcome labels \{Supports, Violates, Indeterminate\} for the candidate Reasoning\,$\rightarrow$\,Legitimacy\,$\rightarrow$\,Execution (RLE) boundary. Consequently, Paper~B2 issues no retained RLE classifications; this limitation is recorded as audit finding \texttt{AUD-001} in Section~\ref{sec:protocol-audit-finding}.

No metric-level cohort patterns are reported in Section~\ref{sec:analysis}. Any future boundary-level classification requires completing the remaining DER, MSR, Comparative Dataset, and Analysis stages; prospectively defining I4/I5 (e.g., in a Protocol v1.x revision or a Paper~B3 registration); and re-executing the full pipeline under those frozen rules.

\paragraph{Freeze rule.}
The registered investigation fields I1--I5 are fixed before execution of
Analysis and Retained Classification. Any change affecting the candidate
invariant, admitted evidence, tolerance, measurement vector, missing-data
handling, or decision function invalidates this execution and requires a new
registered run.

\paragraph{Execution record freeze checklist (post-result).}
After the cohort outcome is determined, freeze the execution record by:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Confirming every retained-classification row traces through Comparative Dataset $\rightarrow$ MSR $\rightarrow$ DER $\rightarrow$ SRF $\rightarrow$ BOR.
  \item Keeping lifecycle wording synchronized with the completed canonical result.
  \item Recording the B2-Run-2 freeze date and source version.
  \item Compiling and exporting the final PDF.
  \item Preserving B2-Run-1 and B2-Run-2 as separate citable executions.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Cohort precedence rule (deterministic and conservative).}
Because the candidate invariant is evaluated over the entire registered cohort,
a single indeterminate system prevents a deterministic cohort-level conclusion.
Therefore, cohort outcomes are ordered by evidential conservatism:
\textit{Indeterminate} $\succ$ \textit{Violates} $\succ$ \textit{Supports}.
This prevents a cohort conclusion from ignoring unresolved evidence.

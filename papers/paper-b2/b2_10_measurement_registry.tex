\section{Measurement Registry}
\label{sec:msr}
The measurement registry (MSR) records protocol-defined measurements computed over derived objects. No Paper~B2 MSR records have been created in the current repository state.

\textbf{Separation rule.} MSR entries must not introduce new observations. Each measurement must cite its DER inputs (and thereby inherits BOR traceability). Any aggregation or normalization performed for a measurement must be described as part of the measurement method.

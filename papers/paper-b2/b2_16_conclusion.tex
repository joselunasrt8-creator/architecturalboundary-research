\section{Conclusion}
Paper~B2 applies the Invariance-Based Architectural Investigation Protocol v1.0 (Paper~0) to a governance/authorization cohort and produces a complete, traceable BOR $\rightarrow$ SRF $\rightarrow$ DER $\rightarrow$ MSR $\rightarrow$ Comparative Dataset chain suitable for replication and metric-level synthesis.

However, the Paper~B2 protocol registration did not preregister a sufficiently explicit investigation-specific I4 measurement mapping and I5 decision function to uniquely map the Comparative Dataset to the Protocol v1.0 outcome labels \{Supports, Violates, Indeterminate\} for the candidate Reasoning\,$\rightarrow$\,Legitimacy\,$\rightarrow$\,Execution (RLE) boundary. Consequently, Paper~B2 issues no retained RLE classifications; this limitation is recorded as audit finding \texttt{AUD-001} in Section~\ref{sec:protocol-audit-finding}.

Metric-level cohort patterns derived solely from the Comparative Dataset are reported in Section~\ref{sec:analysis}. Any future boundary-level classification requires prospectively defining I4/I5 (e.g., in a Protocol v1.x revision or a Paper~B3 registration) and re-executing the full pipeline under those frozen rules.

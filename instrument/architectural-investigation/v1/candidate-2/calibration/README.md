# Instrument v1 Calibration Fixture and Review Protocol

The [fixture](fixture-v1.json) provides bounded classification cases with
predefined expected results. Its purpose is to test whether reviewers apply the
same evidence/authority, maturity, conflict, legitimacy, and promotion rules.

Calibration requires at least two qualified reviewers independent of instrument
authorship. Each reviewer records, before seeing another review, classifications,
rationale, uncertainty, and reasonable disagreements for every case. Exact
agreement is required on controlled values and prohibited inferences. Rationale
differences may remain if they do not change the controlled result. Any result
disagreement requires preserved adjudication by a qualified third reviewer or a
specification revision.

Automation may verify fixture structure and compare recorded values. It may not
create reviewer judgments, decide which rationale is scientifically correct, or
convert one authoring review into independent replication.

The [Issue #107 review](review-record.md) is an internal authoring review only.
The [exemplar mapping](exemplar-mapping.md) preserves the limits of historical
calibration evidence.
